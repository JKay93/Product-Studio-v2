## Outcomes and evidence
Software creation cost can decline without adoption, operation, migration and agreement becoming cheap. Distinguish personal disposable software from shared consequential systems. Observed supply indicators do not establish universal app abundance or AI causation.

- RevenueCat 2026/Appfigures: monthly mobile subscription launches about 2,000 Jan 2022 to 14,700+ Jan 2026. Scope and attribution checked: https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps
- Observational May 2026 preprint: 160,000+ Product Hunt launches, increased solo entry, teams prominent in top rankings. Rankings do not establish revenue/objective quality: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10291
- Buyer-led software collectives: stronger potential economic mechanism, but old collective purchasing with hypothesised lower viable group size. Users control demand, conditions and priorities, not necessarily code ownership. Existing funding/feedback mechanisms overlap (Kickstarter, Algora, Open Collective, Canny). Community formation, agreement and ongoing supplier responsibility remain costly. Initial single-buyer brief is weak differentiation. No validated willingness to pay.
- Shared rule modelling: groups compare consequences under explicit assumptions before choosing rules for shared software. More interesting self-serve interaction, weaker recurrence/payment evidence. Existing alternatives include Loomio, Causal, Ambsheets, airfocus and Smartsheet. Project-prioritisation example illustrates experience, not a verified underserved niche. https://www.inkandswitch.com/project/ambsheets/live25/ ; https://airfocus.com/product/capacity-planning/ ; https://www.smartsheet.com/platform/resource-planning
- Rejected generic app choice/reviews (G2/TrustRadius), proof-of-fit testing (TestBox/prooV), universal ownership/interoperability (Solid), remix infrastructure (Val Town/Websim), app maintenance and generic continuity as primary recommendations. https://docs.val.town/vals/remixes ; https://solidproject.org/faq ; https://www.testbox.com/platform/buyer-sandboxes
