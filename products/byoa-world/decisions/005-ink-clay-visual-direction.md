# Decision 005: professional, slightly playful Ink Clay

- Decision owner and last material update: user; orchestrator records, 2026-10-02.
- Date and authority source: user rejected the Warm Clay colour scheme, requested professionalism with slight playfulness, then accepted the ink/cobalt/periwinkle/apricot recommendation with "sure, i like it" and requested "let's do it. Any changes to roadmap?".
- Constrains: shared visual direction for Stage 2 and its future delivery. Refines only Decision 002's Calm appearance; its functional foundation, stack and preservation rules remain governing.

## Context and decision

Use cool off-white `#F6F7FB`, white work surfaces, deep ink `#202B45`, cobalt primary actions `#3454C5`, periwinkle Offer planner `#E3E8FC`, apricot Operations planner `#FBE6D4`, and slate supporting text `#596579`. Use restrained clay World/agent illustrations, rounded role cards, more white space and less shadow. Keep writing, permissions, review and usage surfaces crisp and flat. UI UX Pro Max is the selected design guidance; task-local installation is not automatic global registration.

Authorize updating the [design record and static concept](../design-system/CLAY-WORLD-PROPOSAL.md) and project planning records. Appearance approval does not accept the complete native participation package or authorize Stage 2.1 application coding, provider preflight, live calls, new spending or weekly pilot resumption. The immediate request concerns the discussed visual revision and roadmap.

## Rationale and consequences

Warm sage/mint/lilac/peach was rejected. Ink and cobalt anchor a professional working environment; periwinkle/apricot and selective clay details retain personality. Decorative identity colours are separate from semantic status; written labels carry meaning. Exact accessible derived tokens and future rendered verification belong to the design record.

Orchestrator maintains roadmap/state authority; Designer revises the static design. Accepted human save/conflict/recovery and existing data/evidence remain intact. Historical foundation and native candidate hashes/reviews retain their original scope; this visual overlay needs its own future rendered checks. No additional roadmap stage is needed: visual refinement belongs to Stage 2 design and approved future UI delivery in 2.1. Full native-package decision, explicit implementation resume and bounded live authority remain outstanding.
