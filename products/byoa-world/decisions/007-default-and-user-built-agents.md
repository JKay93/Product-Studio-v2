# Decision 007: BYOA-built defaults and user-built agents

- Decision owner and last material update: user; orchestrator records, 2026-10-02.
- Date and authority source: the user confirmed "we have default agents made by us, and then we have human made agents" and requested "Note this down" after discussing selectable specialists and replacing defaults with user-created agents.
- Constrains: Incubator, team configuration and later BYOA participation. Complements Decisions004/006; preserves the selected orchestrator → direct sub-agent scope. It does not supersede live, funding, preservation or readiness boundaries.

## Context and decision

BYOA World supports two sources of agents within the same team model:

1. **BYOA-built agents:** ready-made defaults/templates provided by the product so users can begin without designing a team from scratch.
2. **User-built agents:** agents created/configured by people in the Incubator, or brought into the World through a supported BYOA runtime later. “Human made” describes who creates/configures the agent, not a human worker impersonated as an agent.

Teams may mix both sources. A user-built specialist can replace a default in the same role, subject to capability fit and runtime readiness. The agent's source, creator/operator and runtime remain attributable; a common role does not imply identical capabilities, universal external-runtime support or greater trust/access.

Task participation is explicitly selected. The orchestrator works only with the allowed team and may suggest a missing specialist, such as a Designer, for human approval before it joins. It must not silently create, invite or dispatch an unselected agent. Specific interaction details remain for the bounded configuration/task design; the initial direction is a fixed allowed team with suggestions.

Both sources follow the same World/task permission contract, disclosed inputs, company-funding/budget limits and human approval of canonical outputs. Replacing an agent grants no extra knowledge, tools, spending or acceptance authority. Task records retain which actual agent/configuration version contributed; replacement must not rewrite past attribution or silently change an active task. External agents' private sub-agents do not automatically receive World access. Sub-sub-agents and nested delegation remain deferred.

## Rationale and consequences

Defaults reduce setup effort; user-built agents allow specialization and ownership without locking every user into product presets. A common scoped participation contract supports mixed teams while preserving authority and funding boundaries.

Alternatives include default-only teams, external-only onboarding and automatic recruitment of missing expertise. These do not match the confirmed direction: ready-made participation first, user-defined replacements later, and explicit selection before execution.

The suggested Orchestrator/Researcher/Planner/Reviewer lineup remains a proposal, not a frozen default catalogue. Existing Offer planner/Operations planner roles remain the verified offline slice; this decision does not claim general configuration or BYOA onboarding is implemented.
