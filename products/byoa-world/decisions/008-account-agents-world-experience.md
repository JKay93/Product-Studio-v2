# Decision 008: account-owned agents; prove the World experience

- Owner: user; orchestrator records. Updated: 2026-10-03.
- Authority: user supplies “New direction. Understand first” and the five-step build order, then asks to update the roadmap/direction and park remaining work.
- Supersedes: the fixed two-agent-trial-first delivery sequence and World-bound/one-orchestrator configuration restrictions. Refines Decisions 004/006/007 where they conflict; preserves their compatible controls and historical evidence.

## Context and decision

BYOA's end goal is a World where humans and independently owned agents work together. External agents eventually keep their own runtime, model credentials and ownership.

Prove the experience using BYOA-hosted agents first, in this order:

1. **Agent Creation:** account-owned agents, multiple orchestrators, manually created direct sub-agents, and selecting an orchestrator/team for a World.
2. **Chat:** familiar human ↔ orchestrator conversation.
3. **Knowledge:** basic World documents/folders, reading and editing.
4. **Agent Actions:** orchestrator reads authorized knowledge and creates/updates documents.
5. **Sub-agent Collaboration:** delegation, result returned, and orchestrator-produced World work.

An agent is not synonymous with its hosted runtime. Keep agent identity/configuration, runtime execution and World participation/access separate. Hosted and future externally operated agents use common World-facing abstractions for Chat, Knowledge, Work, agent/human interaction and activity. External provider credentials remain with their operator's runtime.

Keep direct orchestrator → sub-agent scope. Authorized orchestrator creation of sub-agents is future work; nested/sub-sub-agents remain deferred. Starter templates may help creation without becoming a mandatory fixed team.

## Rationale and consequences

The prior approach concentrated on a fixed trial rather than the intended everyday World experience. The revised sequence proves useful behavior incrementally. Preserve account identity across Worlds without sharing a World's knowledge or permissions automatically.

Reuse the accepted human foundation, Ink Clay, saved resources, versioning, scoped access, company funding/usage limits, attribution and human review where compatible. Existing World-bound configuration needs redesign; historical outputs/tests do not establish completion of the new milestones.

The bakery trial remains failed and its financial authority consumed. It is not a prerequisite for the new sequence. Validator replacement remains high priority for document actions/collaboration, using instructions and human review for natural writing while retaining technical/access/usage controls.

Orchestrator updates the roadmap, authority/state and backlog. New runtime/ownership implementation design remains future work; this request starts no coding, data migration, provider preflight, model call, new spending, weekly pilot or publication. Deferred candidates stay in the backlog until selected.
