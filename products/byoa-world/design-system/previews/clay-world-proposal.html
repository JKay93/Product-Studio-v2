Corporate lunch-box trial

Plan a Monday–Friday trial for nearby offices. Offer chicken-and-vegetable and vegan-and-vegetable lunch boxes at SGD14 each, with SGD9 variable cost per box.

Minimum 10 boxes per order; maximum 30 per day. We have 150 staff minutes a day, including 30 fixed minutes and 4 minutes per box.

Use 20 boxes a day as a conservative planning scenario: 110 staff minutes and SGD100 contribution. Interested contacts are not confirmed orders.
