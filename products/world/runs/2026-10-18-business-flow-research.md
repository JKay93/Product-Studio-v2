## Google Drive recommendation

Proposal: World would operate one branded OAuth app. SME clicks Connect Google Drive → chooses Google account → selects files through Google Picker → chooses where new outputs go. No customer Cloud Console/API-key setup. Start with non-sensitive `drive.file` per-file authorisation; selected folder is not a promise of recursive access to all pre-existing descendants. All-Drive indexing uses restricted scopes and entails verification/security assessment for server-stored/transmitted restricted data. Store offline refresh tokens encrypted server-side, tied to owner and World; revoke access properly and do not turn private Drive selection into automatic World sharing. Use expiring HTTPS push channels plus reconciliation, not repeated full-library polling. Standard API usage below published thresholds has no additional API charge; current limits page says scaled billing details later in 2026 with 90 days' notice. Storage, hosting and AI remain separate costs. Manual upload/download is the simple fallback, at the cost of automatic update propagation. Two-way rich-format fidelity is not promised for the first connector.

## Singapore invoicing recommendation

InvoiceNow already exists as a Peppol network. Mandatory GST implementation: 1 November 2025 for companies registering voluntarily within six months of incorporation; 1 April 2026 for all new voluntary applicants; 1 April 2028 for new compulsory applicants and existing businesses with annual supplies ≤S$200,000; 2029 for remaining existing ≤S$1m; 2030 for remaining existing ≤S$4m; 2031 for existing >S$4m. Existing thresholds use 2025 Box 4 supplies, not accounting revenue. Normal cohorts exclude overseas entities (including OVR) and businesses liable wholly under reverse charge. Non-GST entities have no GST InvoiceNow mandate under this rollout.

PDF emailed/WhatsApped is not structured InvoiceNow transmission. IRAS reporting adds a fifth corner, covers applicable purchases as well as sales (including non-network counterparties) and does not replace GST returns. World can own structured quote/order/invoice records, invoice UI/PDF, deterministic money/tax calculations, issued versions, linked credit notes and archives; an accredited AP/IRSP handles the network/IRAS connection. Do not become an AP initially or claim accreditation merely by connecting to one. UEN/Peppol registration, Corppass KYC and tax activation remain onboarding requirements. GST invoice fields and at least five years' issued/received invoice retention matter; non-GST entities must not charge GST. Do not blindly apply 9% to exempt/zero-rated cases.

AP API tariff was not verified publicly: quotation needed for setup, per-UEN, volume, IRAS reporting and archiving. Government-listed free packages exist, but individual expiry dates vary (2027/2031), and free SME software does not imply free World API access. Free Zoho Invoice supports ordinary quotes/PDFs; this alone does not prove InvoiceNow compliance. Xero offers InvoiceNow within a paid accounting product. No market-wide adoption statistics claimed; grants are conditional and not included in cost estimates.
