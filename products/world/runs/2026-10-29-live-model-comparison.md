## Findings and recommendation

Recommend **Terra as the first live candidate for company-document preparation and revision**. The higher per-token price produced the strongest observed whole-job result, and cost less than one US cent per accepted job in this small sample. Haiku remains a candidate for simpler read-only questions, subject to a broader matched evaluation before automatic routing. Do not select GLM for document tools on this evidence. No default/model routing or normal-app activation was changed.

Cheaper-model failures show an adapter interaction, not proof that the models are inherently incapable: the rich-document tool rejected proposed payloads, and the draft was not saved. The rejected full arguments were not retained, so exact structural differences are unproven. Haiku also described rejected drafts as prepared and assigned photographing/logging to Leon despite only report receipt being confirmed. GLM acknowledged failed submission more clearly, but repeatedly submitted rejected tool calls. SDK/network retries remain disabled; **model-driven tool repair attempts within the same bounded loop do occur**, and their costs are included. The error latch can make the job fail even after continued generation. Next implementation priorities: a typed field/patch interface that keeps template structure deterministic; explicit recoverable-versus-fatal tool errors with a small repair allowance; and truthful status derived from confirmed saved state. These are recommendations, not implemented during the frozen comparison.
