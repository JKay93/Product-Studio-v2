# Evidence notes and limits

Checked 28 October 2026. The [report](REPORT.md) links claims directly to sources; this note explains confidence, independence and exclusions. 'Verified' means the publication/data was inspected, not that a vendor's claimed outcome was independently reproduced.

| Evidence family | Classification / confidence | Permitted conclusion and principal limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SingStat enterprise landscape](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_f9c93c1ffcefe660272c101cd733711c/view) | Official descriptive data; high confidence in published definitions/counts | Reference2025, updated March2026. Broad SME count is not product eligibility or reachable demand. |
| [SingStat services industries](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_38d62de582eb7ee2c58d1bba4cd4132d/view) | Official descriptive data plus our CSV extraction | Reference2024, published March2026, SSIC2025. The CSV repeats industry labels in several indicator sections; only establishments section was counted. Excludes own-account workers. [Aggregate evidence and hashes](evidence/public-sector-counts-2026-10-28.json). |
| [BCA contractor register](https://data.gov.sg/datasets/d_dcda79be4aded5f9e769b8e23ff69b47/view) | Official historical register plus our deduplication | January2026 snapshot. Unique nonblank UEN, separately any ME workhead. Includes firms/registrations outside proposed target; no current-active, SME, subcontractor or paid-demand inference. |
| [SBF manpower study](https://www.sbf.org.sg/docs/default-source/about-us/sbf-national-business-survey-2026-manpower-wages-supplement.pdf?sfvrsn=6599a99c_1) | Published membership-oriented survey; moderate generalisability | 511 responses, 433 SMEs; July–August2026 fieldwork. Retail/HRA samples too small for reliable segment estimates. SBF press release and articles repeating it are the same evidence family. |
| [SBF annual2025 study](https://www.sbf.org.sg/docs/default-source/advocacy-policy/sbf_abs_2025ea2d87c8-9413-4db6-a82a-070319ac5e75.pdf?sfvrsn=a1d71a07_1) | Published membership-oriented survey; moderate generalisability | 553 responses, 82% SMEs, September–October2025 fieldwork. Mixed-business statistics are not all-SME or microbusiness estimates. Historical context, not labelled latest2026 annual data. |
| [Atradius2026 Singapore](https://atradius.sg/knowledge-and-research/reports/b2b-payment-practices-trends-in-singapore-2026) | Insurer-published survey interpretation; directional | Public page lacks sufficient inspected sample/method detail; no population percentage or sizing multiplier taken into report. Commercial incentive noted. |
| [SIT/SGPC analysis](https://www.singaporetech.edu.sg/news/last-orders-why-are-so-many-singapore-eateries-shutting-down) | Named researcher's interpretation of outlet study; moderate relevance | 380 establishments, several formats; not a sample specifically of independent2–10-outlet groups. SIT republication and original newspaper article are not independent studies. The underlying SGPC PDF exceeded the web tool's file-size fetch limit for the Orchestrator, so no additional uninspected tables are quoted. |
| [MEVO Green-Tech account](https://www.mevo.com.sg/news/green-tech-egg-industries-pte-ltd-to-share-ai-whatsapp-order-automation-success-story-at-smes-go-digital-day-2026/) | Named vendor-selected customer story; low generalisability | Establishes a reported operational example and direct competition. No controlled or independently verified savings/adoption estimate. Future bet's residual failure is still unproven. |
| [BCA payment guidance](https://www1.bca.gov.sg/growth-and-transformation/procurement/procurement-and-legal-frameworks/security-of-payment-act/) | Official process guidance; high confidence in described process | Does not measure documentary failures or guarantee entitlement/payment. Individual contracts and qualified review still matter. |
| [NEA OBC guide](https://www.nea.gov.sg/docs/default-source/itm/obc-guides/guide-on-specifications-for-outcome-based-cleaning-contracts-2026-3rd-edition.pdf) | Official guidance/templates; high confidence in contents | Adaptable examples; not a universal mandate, actual-contract prevalence estimate or willingness-to-pay study. Different sample contracts have different payment bands. |
| [SitePlus](https://siteplus.ai/), [Episcript](https://www.episcript.com/solutions/construction-management-software), [CleanLog](https://cleanlog.com/obc-compliance) | First-party advertised features/prices; reliable as observed offers, outcomes untested | Strong evidence against a claim of feature novelty. No hands-on trial, customer-count validation or market-share measurement. Confirm quotations/billing before purchase. |
| [Foodrazor](https://foodrazor.com/pricing), [billing help](https://help.foodrazor.com/en/articles/17239512-manage-your-billing), [Nomni](https://support.zeemart.co/en/articles/9418004-using-the-recipes-feature) | First-party product documentation; outcomes untested | Pricing currency is not inferred from a Singapore audience. Foodrazor automation allowance units differ across inspected pages. Do not silently reconcile the discrepancy. |
| [Xero](https://developer.xero.com/pricing), [WhatsApp policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) | First-party access/commercial rules; current at inspection | API existence is not customer authorisation, commercial approval or zero-cost access. No account connection or operational API test performed. |

Additional sources used only for rejected-direction screening are linked in the report. Public forum complaints were exploratory leads, not verified owner interviews, and were not used to estimate incidence, losses or demand. An IMDA company-profile link encountered an anti-bot challenge in the Orchestrator's source check and was excluded as a pillar of the final argument.

No desk source proves the proposed unmet competitive gaps, prices customers will pay us, acquisition cost, retention or a defensible moat. Proposed journeys, economic examples, target segments, technical approaches and interview gates are analysis/hypotheses. The study remains valid even if interviews reject every candidate.
