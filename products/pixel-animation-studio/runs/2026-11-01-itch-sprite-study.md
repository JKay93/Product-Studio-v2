- Prior emphasis onrecovery/resizing solvedrasterhygiene, not spriteartdirection. GeneratedBram startedlike a detailedstandingcharacter illustration; reducingit cannot substitute for designing a smallgamecharacter fromstart.
- Separate environmenttilegrid, occupiedcharacterbounds, animationcell/pivot, atlas dimensions andexportdisplayscale. ManaSeed32pxbody/64cell andCozyPeople20x16body/32cell are concrete examples. Blankcellspace supports tools/hair/actionextent, not wastedpixels. Spriteframe mustnot beforced tofilldeliverycanvas.
